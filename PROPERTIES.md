# PACT — Fuzzing Properties

English specification of every property the Fizz suite asserts about `Offering`,
`PactToken` and `OfferingFactory`. IDs are **stable** — never renumber them; new
properties append at the end of their section.

**Checkbox meaning**

- `[ ]` — specified, not yet implemented in `contracts/test/fizz/Properties.sol`
- `[x]` — implemented and wired into the harness
- `[-]` — deliberately skipped (the reason is stated inline)

**Guarantee tag**

- **SHOULD-HOLD** — backed by concrete evidence (a documented invariant, a guard,
  or an exact identity in the source). A failure is a bug in the protocol.
- **EXPLORATORY** — a plausible-but-unproven expectation. A failure may be a bug
  _or_ a correction to the expectation; several are deliberately falsifiable and
  the counterexample is the deliverable.

`Sources` cites the discovery agents' raw property IDs
(`contracts/fizz_data/discovery/`); the implementation tables live in
`contracts/fizz_data/property-plan.md`.
